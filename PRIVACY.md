# Privacy Policy — EditIn

**Last Updated:** November 22, 2026

**EditIn** ("we", "our", or "the application"), developed by **Ngoods Studio**, respects your privacy.

## 1. Data Collection
**EditIn does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user files.**

## 2. Offline Processing
All PDF editing, page management, merging, splitting, signing, compression, and password protection operations are performed **100% locally** on your device. Your documents never leave your computer.

## 3. Third-Party Services
EditIn does not use analytics, tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or external cloud APIs.
